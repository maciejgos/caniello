# Story: Landing Page Future Waves Research Framing

## Status

Accepted

Human acceptance note: accepted by explicit human instruction in this Codex session after `product_coherence_reviewer` returned `valid to accept`.

## User Story

As a visitor interested in Caniello's broader vision, I want Wave 2 and Wave 3 to be clearly marked as research, so that I understand what is being explored without mistaking it for an available product.

## Background

Caniello should communicate future pet services and clinic collaboration lightly while preserving Wave 1 owner-side MVP validation. The landing page must not over-promise marketplace or clinic capabilities.

## Scope

- What's next section explaining that Caniello starts with health records, reminders, documents, and trusted sharing.
- Wave 2 pet services card or section labeled research, exploration, or equivalent.
- Wave 3 clinic collaboration card or section labeled research, exploration, or equivalent.
- Copy stating that future waves depend on validated demand.
- Navigation behavior that avoids presenting marketplace or clinic collaboration as active MVP products.
- Research-stage CTA labels for interested providers and clinics without owning the submission or routing behavior.

## Out of Scope

- Dedicated Wave 2 or Wave 3 product pages unless explicitly selected in design.
- Marketplace search, booking, payment, provider profiles, or transactions.
- Clinic SaaS, clinical workflows, clinic integrations, or provider portals.
- Public roadmap promises or launch dates.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given the page presents future vision, when a visitor reads the section, then Wave 2 and Wave 3 are labeled as research, exploration, or future waves dependent on validated demand.
- Given the Wave 2 section is displayed, when a provider reads it, then it says Caniello is researching a curated way for pet owners to discover trusted pet-related services and providers.
- Given the Wave 3 section is displayed, when a clinic or vet reads it, then it says Caniello is exploring tools that help clinics and vets work with owner-approved pet health context.
- Given a visitor scans the page navigation, when they look for active product areas, then marketplace and clinic capabilities are not presented as primary active MVP products.
- Given the page copy is reviewed, when Wave 2 or Wave 3 is mentioned, then it does not use `coming soon`, `launching next`, `available now`, or equivalent launch-commitment language.
- Given a future-wave CTA is displayed, when a visitor reads the CTA label and nearby copy, then the action is framed as research interest rather than product access.
