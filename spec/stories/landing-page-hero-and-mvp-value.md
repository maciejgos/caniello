# Story: Landing Page Hero and MVP Value

## Status

## User Story

As a pet owner, I want to quickly understand Caniello's core value, so that I can decide whether it is relevant enough to join the waitlist.

## Background

The landing page must keep Wave 1 owner-side validation as the center of gravity. The first viewport and value section need to explain Caniello as one place for pet health records, reminders, documents, and owner-controlled sharing.

## Scope

- Hero section with Caniello branding, owner-side headline, short product description, primary waitlist action, and privacy cue.
- Visual direction using app/product mockup and pet imagery consistent with the landing concept images.
- MVP value section with health records, smart reminders, care sharing, and secure/private positioning.
- Simple process section explaining add your pet, organize care, and stay on top.
- Scope-safe copy for records, reminders, documents, privacy, and sharing.

## Out of Scope

- Functional lead submission beyond connecting the hero CTA to the shared waitlist form behavior.
- Authenticated product functionality.
- Building pet profiles, reminders, document vault, care summary, or sharing features.
- Wave 2 or Wave 3 interest capture.
- Final legal approval of marketing copy.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given a first-time pet owner visitor, when the landing page loads, then the first viewport identifies Caniello as a pet health and care organization utility.
- Given a visitor reads the hero, when they scan the headline and supporting copy, then they can understand the promise of health records, reminders, documents, and trusted sharing.
- Given a visitor views the hero on a common desktop viewport, when they decide to act, then a waitlist CTA is visible without scrolling.
- Given a visitor scans the MVP value section, when they compare the cards or blocks, then they see health records, smart reminders, care sharing, and secure/private positioning.
- Given the page describes health records, when it mentions medical history, vaccinations, tests, or documents, then it does not claim to replace an official EU pet passport.
- Given the page describes reminders, when it mentions medication, vaccines, appointments, deworming, or visits, then it does not imply diagnosis or medical advice.
- Given the page describes care sharing, when it mentions family, caregivers, or vets, then it explains that sharing is owner-controlled.
- Given a visitor reads the process section, when they evaluate how to start, then they see a simple three-step flow for adding a pet, organizing care, and staying on top.
