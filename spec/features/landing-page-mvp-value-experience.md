# Feature: Landing Page MVP Value Experience

## Status

Accepted

Human acceptance note: accepted by explicit human instruction in this Codex session to accept the landing page features using `product_coherence_reviewer`.

## Summary

Present a polished Caniello landing page experience that helps pet owners understand the Wave 1 MVP promise, trust the product positioning, and find a clear waitlist action without implying that future marketplace or clinic capabilities are available now.

## Source Requirements

- [Landing Page Segmented Validation Requirement](../requirements/landing-page-segmented-validation.md)
- [First Landing Page Requirement](../requirements/first-landing-page.md)

## Users and Use Cases

- Pet owners quickly assess whether Caniello helps organize pet health records, reminders, documents, and care sharing.
- Visitors scan the page to understand how Caniello works before deciding whether to join the waitlist.
- The founder validates whether the landing page communicates the owner-side MVP promise clearly.
- Future implementation agents use a stable feature boundary for the page structure and messaging scope.

## Scope

- Hero section with the primary owner-side promise, app/product mockup direction, pet imagery, and primary waitlist CTA.
- MVP value section covering health records, smart reminders, care sharing, and secure/private positioning.
- Simple process section explaining add your pet, organize care, and stay on top.
- Mid-page or lower-page waitlist CTA reinforcing the owner value after the product explanation.
- What's next section that references Wave 2 and Wave 3 as research or exploration.
- Footer with product, company, resource, legal, and final signup access.
- Responsive desktop and mobile presentation that follows the landing concept images as design direction.

## Out of Scope

- Authenticated product functionality.
- Pet profile creation, reminder management, document vault, care summary generation, or sharing flows.
- Active marketplace, booking, payments, provider portal, clinic portal, or clinic integrations.
- AI diagnosis, symptom checking, treatment recommendations, or veterinary decision-making.
- Official EU pet passport replacement claims.
- Final approved production copy beyond the constraints and message intent captured in the source requirements.

## Behavior

Visitors can understand Caniello's MVP promise from the first viewport, scan the core owner value, learn the simple start process, and see future directions framed as research. The page keeps owner-side validation central while making waitlist actions available in the hero, mid or lower page, and footer.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given a first-time pet owner visitor, when they open the landing page, then they see `Better care. Stronger bond.` or equivalent approved owner-side promise above the fold.
- Given a first-time visitor, when they scan the first viewport, then they can identify that Caniello helps with health records, reminders, documents, and trusted care sharing.
- Given a visitor views the hero, when they decide to act, then they can find a visible waitlist CTA without scrolling on common desktop layouts.
- Given a visitor reads the MVP value section, when they compare the feature cards, then they see health records, smart reminders, care sharing, and secure/private positioning.
- Given the page describes health records, when it mentions medical history, vaccinations, tests, or documents, then it does not claim to replace an official EU pet passport.
- Given the page describes reminders, when it mentions medication, vaccines, deworming, appointments, or visits, then it does not imply diagnosis or medical advice.
- Given the page describes care sharing, when it mentions family, caregivers, or vets, then it explains that sharing is owner-controlled.
- Given a visitor reads the process section, when they evaluate effort to start, then they see a simple flow for adding a pet, organizing care, and staying on top.
- Given the page references Wave 2 or Wave 3, when a visitor reads those sections, then they are labeled as research, exploration, or future waves dependent on validated demand.
- Given the navigation is displayed, when a visitor scans available sections, then marketplace and clinic capabilities are not presented as active MVP products.
- Given the footer is displayed, when a visitor looks for product or company links, then they can access the main product, company, resource, and legal destinations or clearly marked placeholders before publication.
- Given the footer or final signup area is displayed, when a visitor decides to join the waitlist at the end of the page, then a final signup action is available without returning to the hero.
- Given the page is viewed on common mobile and desktop sizes, when a visitor scans the hero, sections, CTAs, and footer, then text remains readable and controls remain usable without incoherent overlap.
